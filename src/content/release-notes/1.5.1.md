## What's new in 1.5.1

One fix: when a dictation fails to paste, Handy Plus now tells you.

- **A paste that silently goes nowhere is no longer silent.** Occasionally the
  text simply does not appear in the app you were dictating into. macOS accepts
  the keystroke and reports success, the application quietly ignores it, and
  nothing anywhere raises an error — so the first you know of it is the missing
  text. Handy Plus could already tell the difference between a paste that landed
  and one that vanished; it just kept that to itself. It now says so, so you can
  paste again immediately instead of wondering.

This needs **Reliable paste** switched on (Debug settings), because that is the
only mode that can tell whether the text was actually read. Pairing it with
Settings → Advanced → Clipboard Handling → "Copy to Clipboard" means a failed
paste costs you one Cmd+V, since the transcript is still on your clipboard.

This does not stop the paste from failing — that cause is still being chased.
It makes the failure visible, and recorded, which is what finding the cause
needs.
