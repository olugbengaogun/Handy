## What's new in 1.5.3

Handy Plus catches up with upstream Handy, and picks up a new way to decide how your dictation shortcut behaves.

### Pick how your shortcut works

General settings now has **Shortcut Behavior**, with three choices:

- **Auto** — hold the shortcut to dictate, or tap it to leave recording running until you tap again. Handy Plus works out which you meant from how long you held the key.
- **Hold** — records only while the shortcut is held, exactly as push-to-talk did.
- **Toggle** — tap to start, tap again to stop.

Nothing changes for you unless you want it to: if you had push-to-talk switched on it becomes **Hold**, and if you had it off it becomes **Toggle**. **Auto** is the starting point for new installs, and you can switch to it any time. If you try Auto and short holds keep leaving recording on — or deliberate taps keep getting treated as holds — there is a **Hold Threshold** in Debug settings to tune where the line sits.

### Fixes

- **Push-to-talk could stick on.** Letting go of the shortcut sometimes left recording running, if Handy Plus happened to re-register its shortcuts while you were still holding the key. The release was going to a registration that had just been replaced, so nothing was listening for it.
- **Norwegian and Irish are now in the transcription language list.** Norwegian was missing from it entirely, and Tagalog is now labelled Filipino (Tagalog).
- **`--start-hidden` really starts hidden.** Launching with `--start-hidden` or `--no-tray` on the command line now decides whether Handy Plus takes a Dock icon, instead of only the saved setting deciding — and that decision is made before the app appears rather than after it has already started.
- **Paste delay goes up to 5 seconds.** The old ceiling was half a second, which was not enough for applications that are slow to accept pasted text.
