## What's new in 1.1.3

This release is about trust: making sure that when Handy Plus tidies up what you
said, it can never quietly change what you meant.

- **AI cleanup can no longer alter a figure.** If you have post-processing
  switched on and the cleaned-up version changes an amount, a date or a
  quantity you actually said — or drops a link or an email address — Handy Plus
  now refuses it and keeps your original words. Reformatting is still welcome:
  saying "eighty thousand naira" and getting `₦80,000` is the same number, and
  that still comes through.
- **Very long dictation is checked too.** Cleanup results were previously
  accepted without review once a transcript grew past a couple of thousand
  words. They are now held to the same standard as everything else.
- **Correcting a transcript keeps it consistent.** When you fix a transcript in
  History, the old cleaned-up copy of the previous version is no longer kept
  alongside your correction.
- **Your corrections now count for something.** Fixing a transcript marks it as
  checked by you. Nothing about this leaves your Mac — it simply lets the
  built-in accuracy benchmark measure against transcripts a person has actually
  read, instead of against text nobody ever looked at.

Everything here runs on your Mac, and none of it changes how transcription
itself works or how fast it is.
