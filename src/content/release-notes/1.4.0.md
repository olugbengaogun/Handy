## What's new in 1.4.0

Your music gets out of the way while you talk, and the Insights panel finally
opens on the week you are actually in.

- **Your music pauses while you dictate, and picks up where it left off.**
  Handy Plus could already mute your Mac's output while recording, which stops
  background audio leaking into the microphone but leaves the track running —
  so you come back mid-chorus. Turn on Settings → Sound → Pause Music While
  Recording and Spotify is paused instead, then resumed from the same spot the
  moment you stop. It only ever undoes its own work: if the music was already
  paused, or you stopped it yourself mid-recording, it is left alone. Spotify
  only for now, and macOS will ask your permission to control it the first time
  you switch the setting on.
- **Insights opens on this week instead of last year.** The activity calendar
  holds a year of squares, which is wider than the panel, and it was starting at
  the far left — so opening Insights showed a wall of empty days and you had to
  scroll sideways to find anything you had actually done. It now starts on the
  most recent week, with the history running off to the left.
- **Your current streak was wrong, and read 0.** The calendar was counting days
  that had not happened yet — the rest of the current week, month or year — and
  an empty tomorrow was enough to break the run. Every day you had dictated was
  recorded correctly; only the number on top of it was wrong. It now counts up
  to today and stops there, so the streak reflects what you have really done.

Transcription itself is unchanged, including its speed and accuracy. Pausing
your music happens entirely on your Mac, and nothing about what you play is
recorded or sent anywhere.
