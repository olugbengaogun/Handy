## What's new in 1.3.0

Two things this release: your transcripts read a little more like you meant
them, and you can finally get them out of the app.

- **"You know" and "I mean" no longer clutter your transcripts.** Speech is full
  of them and writing is not, so Handy Plus now drops them — but only where you
  clearly used them as filler, which it can tell because you paused. "Do you
  know the answer" and "I mean it" are left exactly as you said them. It happens
  the moment you stop recording, costs no time at all, and needs no account, no
  key and no internet. English only for now; you can turn it off under Settings →
  Advanced.
- **You can export your transcripts.** Pick a date range, or tick exactly the
  ones you want, and save them as Markdown to read, CSV to open in a spreadsheet,
  or plain text to use anywhere. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you choose where
  to put the file.
- **There is also an export made for training.** If you ever want to teach a
  model to clean up speech the way you do, Handy Plus can hand you every
  correction you have made as a JSONL file. From this release onward it also
  keeps the original wording behind each transcript you correct, so the pairs
  build up as you work. Corrections made before today cannot be included — the
  original text was replaced at the time and is not recoverable.
- **The export tells you when your history has been trimmed.** Handy Plus only
  keeps a set number of transcripts by default and deletes the rest, which is
  easy to forget until an export comes back short. If that setting is limiting
  what you can export, the dialog now says so before you save.

Transcription itself is unchanged, including its speed and accuracy. Everything
here runs on your Mac, and an export goes only to the file you pick.
