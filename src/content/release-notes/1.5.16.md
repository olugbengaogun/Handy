## What's new in 1.5.16

This update brings the latest improvements from the upstream Handy project into Handy Plus.

- **Chinese script setting.** Choose whether Chinese transcriptions come out in Simplified or Traditional characters (Advanced → Transcription). Other languages are never changed. If you had picked "Chinese (Simplified)" or "Chinese (Traditional)" as your language, it carries over automatically.
- **Cancel shortcut fix.** After a very short recording, the cancel shortcut could stay registered and keep capturing its key. It now always releases.
- **Custom sounds refresh.** Opening the sound picker now picks up custom sound files you've added or removed.
- **Updated transcription engine.** transcribe-cpp moves to 0.3.0.
- **Windows installer** now shows the Handy Plus icon.
