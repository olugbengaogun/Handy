## What's new in 1.2.0

Insights was showing you the same three numbers no matter which button you
pressed. This release is mostly about making it tell the truth — and then
making it worth looking at.

- **Your usage history is now kept properly.** Insights used to count from the
  transcript list, which is trimmed as it grows. Past a few days of real use
  that meant "this week", "this month" and "all time" were all reading the same
  surviving handful of entries and returning identical figures. A small daily
  record is now kept separately and never trimmed, so the numbers are real and
  stay real. Whatever your existing history still holds has been carried over.
- **You can look at any period, not just now.** Day, week, month, year, or
  everything — with arrows to step back through them. And the labels mean what
  they say: "This Month" used to quietly mean "the last 30 days", which on the
  3rd of the month was mostly showing you last month.
- **Insights actually shows something.** Words per day, a year-at-a-glance
  calendar with your current and longest run of consecutive days, time spent
  speaking, and your speaking pace. No leaderboards and no percentile — Handy
  Plus has nobody to compare you against, and a made-up rank is worth less than
  no rank.
- **Corrections you have made once are no longer invisible.** A fix has always
  needed to happen twice before Handy Plus offers to apply it automatically —
  that is what stops a one-off typo becoming a permanent rule. But the first
  sighting was recorded and shown nowhere, so correcting a transcript looked
  like it had been ignored. Those now appear under "Still learning".
- **"I'm" is no longer suggested as a word to add to your dictionary.**
  Contractions were being mistaken for names, so I'm, don't, that's and their
  relatives were cluttering the list of terms worth teaching.
- **Quitting no longer reports a crash.** Handy Plus was shutting down cleanly
  and then tripping over on the way out, so macOS showed a "quit unexpectedly"
  report for an ordinary Cmd-Q. Nothing was ever lost, but nothing about it
  looked reassuring either.
- **A setting changed just before quitting is no longer lost.** Settings were
  written a fraction of a second after you changed them; quitting inside that
  window discarded the change.
- **You can choose which input channel to record from**, for microphones and
  audio interfaces that carry more than one.

Transcription itself is unchanged in this release, including its speed and
accuracy. Everything in Insights is counted on your Mac from your own history
and goes nowhere else.
